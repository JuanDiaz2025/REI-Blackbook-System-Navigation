# Lead Note Template (use on every touch)

Paste this into the **Notes** tab on the lead record in REI BlackBook on **every** call, text, email, or voicemail — inbound or outbound. It matches the `CALL SUMMARY` / `++` bullet style already used in the system (so it drops straight into the existing Notes feed), plus the two fields that are usually missing: a current **Situation** snapshot and a dated **Next Step**.

```
CALL SUMMARY – [Date]
++ Contact Result: [Answered/No Answer/VM/Text — inbound or outbound — duration if relevant]
++ Situation: [one line: current state of this deal, right now]
++ Discussed: [key points raised this touch]
++ Next Step: [specific action] — Due: [date] — Owner: [you / them / teammate name]
++ Status: [lead temperature/stage, matches the badge at top of the contact]
```

## Filled-out example

```
CALL SUMMARY – Jul 31, 2026
++ Contact Result: Answered (outbound, ~6 min)
++ Situation: Seller inherited property, deciding between listing vs. cash sale.
++ Discussed: Timeline (needs to close by Sept), condition (roof issue), price expectation ($180k).
++ Next Step: Send cash offer range by email today — Due: 07/31/2026 — Owner: me. Callback to confirm receipt — Due: 08/04/2026 — Owner: me.
++ Status: Warm — comparing options
```

Compare to a bare `CALL SUMMARY` note with just Contact Result + Summary (no Situation/Next Step) — that's the pattern to move away from: it tells you what happened, but not what to do about it or where things stand.

## Rules of thumb

1. **One note per touch, no exceptions.** If nothing changed, still log it ("No Answer, left VM, will retry Thu").
2. **Situation is not history** — it's the *current* snapshot. Overwrite your mental model of "where this deal is" every time, don't just append to a growing story.
3. **Next Step must be concrete and dated**, and match a real reminder in the **Tasks** tab. "Follow up" is not a next step; "Call back Thu 8/6 to confirm inspection date" is.
4. **Status drives the pipeline/temperature badge** — keep it current so a glance at the contact list tells you who's hot without opening every record.
5. Mid-call, if you can only jot two things, make them **Situation** and **Next Step** — those are the non-negotiable minimum; backfill Contact Result/Discussed right after.
6. If a colleague is picking up this lead (see the "hey, can you grab this lead" style notes), the Next Step field is exactly where the handoff instruction belongs — so it's next to the situation, not a separate message.
